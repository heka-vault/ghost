# Month close

Run this on the first working day of the month, or when the operator says "close the books".

1. Reconcile every account against its statement. Name any difference and stop if one is unexplained.
2. Post the recurring entries for the month.
3. Review open invoices: which are overdue, by how long, and who to chase.
4. Produce the month's summary: revenue, expenses, cash, and the three largest changes against last month.

Each step reads before it writes; a step that fails stops the close, and the operator hears which one.
