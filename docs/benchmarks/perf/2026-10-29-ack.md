# Perf: the acknowledgment before a call to an app (2026-10-29)

heka-vault/ghost#43 adds a step to some turns: when the model's reply begins with a call to
a connected app, Ghost plays a one- or two-word clip ("Sure.", "On it.") rendered ahead in
its voice, then the tools run and the answer follows. ADR 0018 asks what that costs.

Measured with `make perf RUNS=3 ARGS=--no-jev` on the workstation, the same harness and
models as [the Rust pipeline's first report](2026-10-29-rust-voice.md): the baseline on the
commit before the change (e8bfc20), then the branch, then the branch with the clips trimmed
to their words (Kokoro pads a short phrase with about 0.2 s of silence). Seconds, p50 of 3
runs, from the end of the operator's speech:

| scenario | reply before | reply, untrimmed clips | reply, trimmed clips | first sound before | first sound after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lookup | 1.61 | 1.86 | 1.83 | 1.65 | 0.99 |
| announce | 1.57 | 1.82 | 1.66 | 1.43 | 0.99 |
| confirm_plain | 1.41 | 1.80 | 1.67 | 1.33 | 0.89 |
| chain | 2.35 | 2.02 | 2.05 | 2.25 | 0.96 |
| session | 1.45 | 1.82 | 1.66 | 1.47 | 0.92 |
| small_talk (no app) | 1.80 | 1.78 | 1.78 | 1.62 | 1.49 |

"First sound" is the report's `audible` column: when Ghost's voice is first loud at the
scripted speaker. With the acknowledgment the operator hears Ghost 0.4 to 1.3 s sooner on
every turn that goes to an app. The new `ack` figure (end of speech to the clip's first
sample) is 1.05 s at p50 and p95 here: the model's first token is at 0 ms in this harness,
so it is the hearing stage (1.04 s) plus the hop.

**What it costs.** Turns that call an app answer 0.1 to 0.25 s later in this harness, and
only here: the scripted model has the answer ready at once, so it waits for the clip to
finish rather than talk over it. With the real model the tool call and the second model
call take seconds (the operator's turns on 2026-10-29: tools 0.6 to 2.7 s, then a second
call of 1 to 3 s), and the clip, 0.47 to 0.71 s trimmed, plays inside that wait. Turns that
call no app are unchanged (small_talk, and `reply, answered directly` 1.34 s before and
1.29 s after across all scenarios). Every scenario ran 3/3 in every run; the three
`interrupted` turns are `lookup_pause`'s, before and after.

Rendering is off the turn's path: each voice's eight clips are rendered once, on a thread of
their own after the models load, and kept in `~/.cache/ghost/acks-v2/`.
